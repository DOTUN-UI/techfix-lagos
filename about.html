About TechFix Lagos

A small repair shop in Computer Village that explains every fix before it starts.

Our story

TechFix Lagos started as a single bench in Computer Village, Ikeja, fixing phones for neighbours and friends. People came back because we told them what was wrong, showed them the part and charged a fair price.

Today we repair laptops and phones for students, offices and families across Lagos.

Our mission

To get your device working again quickly, with honest advice and parts you can trust, and to stand behind every repair with a 30-day warranty.

Our team

Lead technician

Handles laptop board repairs and data recovery.

Phone repair specialist

Screens, batteries and charging ports, usually the same day.

Customer care

Gives quotes, updates you on progress and handles pickups.
