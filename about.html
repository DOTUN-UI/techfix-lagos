About TechFix Lagos

Our story

TechFix Lagos started in 2019 as a small bench in Computer Village, Ikeja. We began by fixing friends' phones and grew by word of mouth because we always told customers the truth about what was wrong and what it would cost.

Today our team repairs hundreds of laptops and phones every month for students, offices and families across Lagos.

Our mission

To make reliable technology repairs affordable, fast and easy to trust.

  • Be honest about every fault and price
  • Respect our customers' time and data
  • Stand behind our work with a warranty
